About the Workshop

As AI evolves from isolated LLMs to collaborative multi-agent systems, the networking landscape faces an unprecedented paradigm shift. The Internet of Agents (IoA) envisions a distributed ecosystem where autonomous agents continuously communicate, negotiate, and synchronize — generating highly specific traffic patterns that challenge traditional network infrastructures. NIPA 2026 brings together researchers and experts from academia and industry to share the latest research, identify gaps, and define the roadmap for agent-native networking.
