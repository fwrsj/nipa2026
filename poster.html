About the Workshop

As AI evolves from isolated LLMs to collaborative multi-agent systems, the networking landscape faces an unprecedented paradigm shift. The Internet of Agents (IoA) envisions a distributed ecosystem where autonomous agents continuously communicate, negotiate, and synchronize — generating traffic patterns that challenge traditional infrastructures. NIPA 2026 brings together researchers from academia and industry to share the latest research and define the roadmap for agent-native networking.
